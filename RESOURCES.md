# English Learning Resources

Curated registry for source-backed teaching. This is not learner progress state.

## Knowledge

- [Council of Europe CEFR descriptors](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors) and [Companion Volume](https://book.coe.int/en/education-and-modern-languages/8152-common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching-assessment-companion-volume.html) — authoritative can-do, interaction, mediation, listening/reading, and phonological-control framing. Use for calibration and task design, not a curriculum sequence. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [Cambridge Learner's Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/learner-english/) — learner definitions, examples, collocations, and audio. Use for observed grammar/usage/vocabulary questions; dictionary meaning is not frequency evidence. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [Oxford 3000 selection method](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/about/oxford3000) — corpus frequency, dispersion, and expert-judgment signal for usefulness. Use as one vocabulary prioritization signal, not an acquisition measure. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [English Vocabulary Profile](https://www.englishprofile.org/wordlists/evp) — candidate meanings, phrases, and CEFR bands. Use for prioritization and sense selection, not proof of mastery. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [British Council LearnEnglish](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/) — institutional listening and language-learning materials. Select individual items for comprehensibility, transcript/audio quality, length, accent, and current capability relevance; do not treat the collection as a fixed course. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [Karpicke & Roediger (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1152408) — primary retrieval-practice evidence relevant to delayed retention. Use with scope and population noted; does not define a universal schedule. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [Kim & Webb (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12479) — second-language spacing synthesis. Supports adaptive spacing and delayed tests, not fixed intervals for every item. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [Council of Europe phonological competence](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/phonological-competence) — intelligibility/comprehensibility-oriented pronunciation reference. Accessed 2026-08-16.
- [ETS TOEIC workplace task descriptions](https://www.ets.org/toeic/about/bridge.html) — workplace task-shape examples only; not a curriculum or proxy for engineering team-call readiness. Accessed 2026-08-16.

## Domain sources

- Official JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Git, testing, and CI/CD documentation may be selected when a Work capability needs authentic technical context. Choose the exact source per task and check recency.

## Source policy

Prefer standards, primary research/reviews, corpus-backed references, institutional media, and authentic domain sources. Lookup is required for contested, current, or high-impact claims; ordinary generated practice does not require new research. Record reusable sources here with purpose, authority, scope, limitations, provenance, and access date. Do not copy long copyrighted text or transcripts.

## Gaps

- No permanent listening item is selected before the baseline identifies the useful difficulty, support, transcript, and duration.
- Technical/workplace media will be added only when a real bounded objective needs a reusable source.
