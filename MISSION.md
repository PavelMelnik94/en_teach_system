# Mission: Functional English for Life and International Frontend Work

## Why

Build practical English for life in Tbilisi, social interaction, and working in an English-speaking frontend team, including voice calls, HR conversations, and technical interviews. The aim is fastest sustainable functional progress: evidence of real communication, not completion of a fixed course.

## Success looks like

- Handle ordinary and unexpected Life situations, including describing unknown things, clarifying, repairing misunderstandings, and reaching an actionable outcome.
- Start and sustain Social interactions, answer unexpected questions, tell short stories, express opinions, and recover communication breakdowns.
- Understand and participate in progressively more realistic Work conversations: status, blockers, clarification, bugs, reasoning, trade-offs, follow-ups, and team calls.
- Conduct recruiter, HR, project, and technical interview conversations without depending on memorized answers or switching to Russian.

## Constraints

- Native language: Russian. Early grammar, instructions, and error explanations should be primarily in Russian; English exposure increases with comprehension.
- Teaching-language policy: grammar explanations, task instructions, feedback, and error analysis are in Russian at the current stage. English is used for target examples, phrases, sentences, and direct language practice, with Russian translation or clarification when needed. Reduce Russian instructional support only when comprehension evidence supports it.
- Current level is only a self-reported A1 hypothesis. Previous learning included tutors, Duolingo, LinguaLeo, self-study grammar, and Skyeng A1→A2; LinguaLeo felt most useful.
- Listening and speaking are priority modes; reading supports them, and writing is secondary.
- Sessions should work in about 10–40 minutes and adapt to evidence, cognitive load, available modality, and real-life relevance.
- Use `/teach` persistent artifacts as source of truth. Mastery is slice-level evidence, not lesson completion or perfection; do not create a fixed A1→B2 curriculum or depend on paper textbooks.

## Out of scope

- A prewritten year-long curriculum, fixed lesson sequence, or English-only immersion.
- A separate teaching engine, database, scheduler, or parallel learner model.
