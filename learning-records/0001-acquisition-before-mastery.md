# Acquisition must precede mastery checks

Correct answers produced while a rule or example remains visible are guided/open-reference evidence, not independent acquisition. For foundational grammar, `/teach` must first build and check a simple mental model in Russian, then fade support before closed-book retrieval, transfer, or delayed review.

**Evidence:** In the first `live in / be from` session, pattern matching and rule copying produced superficially correct answers while the learner explicitly reported not understanding `be`, `am/is/are`, or why English needs a verb.

**Implication:** Never infer mastery from answer accuracy alone; learner-reported non-understanding lowers confidence and can reveal a prerequisite blocker.
