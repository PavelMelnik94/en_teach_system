# `and` can join descriptions that share one `be`

The learner corrected the initial idea that `and` always separates two independent clauses. After one clarification, he independently produced both `She is a developer and she is tired` and `She is a developer and tired`, showing immediate transfer between a repeated subject-plus-verb clause and two descriptions sharing one linking verb; delayed retention remains untested.

Voice follow-up remained supported rather than independent: both forms were eventually produced, but the full model was needed to repair word order and the article. `she is` and `tired` were intelligible; the next check must preserve the exact sentence pair and remove visible text.
