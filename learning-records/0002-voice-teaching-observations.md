# Voice teaching observations

Short programming/React analogies materially improve comprehension when a foundational grammar explanation is not clicking. Use them selectively, not as a compulsory teaching style.

The learner becomes overloaded when foundational grammar, collocations, and exceptions are introduced together. Keep one small mental model in focus and defer unrelated usage details.

Voice sessions should include a small pronunciation/intelligibility check whenever pedagogically reasonable, while recording `not tested` when it was not actually assessed.
